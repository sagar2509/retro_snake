Privacy Policy for Retro Snake

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Retro Snake ("the app") is a single-player arcade game. This policy explains what data, if any, the app collects.

Data Collection

Retro Snake does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, does not access the internet, and does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs).

Locally Stored Data

The app saves your high score on your device only, using standard local device storage. This information never leaves your device, is not shared with the developer or any third party, and is removed automatically if you uninstall the app.

Permissions

Retro Snake does not request any special device permissions (such as location, camera, microphone, or contacts).

Children's Privacy

Since the app collects no personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
